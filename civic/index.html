Our AI guardrails

Built into every engagement, not added later.

  • Human in the loop. AI never submits, approves, denies or pays anything without a person's decision.
  • Least privilege. Read-only access by default, with every permission tied to a stated purpose.
  • Full audit trail. Every prompt, data access and action is logged for oversight and public records.
  • Clear disclosure. The public always knows when they are interacting with AI, in line with the Texas Responsible AI Governance Act (HB 149).
  • Your data stays yours. Private model deployments in authorized clouds, and no training on constituent data.
  • Measured risk. Use cases assessed against the NIST AI Risk Management Framework before launch.

Spotlight  ·  AI Queue Management

Shorter lines at the DMV, tax office and courthouse.

Long waits at vehicle registration, driver license, property tax and court counters are among the most visible frustrations people have with government. AI can cut the wait without adding staff, while keeping every decision with your team.

  1. Book or check in from anywhereOnline appointments, walk-in check-in by text or QR code, and a live estimated wait time.
  2. Arrive with the right documentsAn AI assistant confirms the visit reason and checks required documents, so fewer trips end in "come back with proof of insurance."
  3. Skip the trip entirelySimple tasks such as renewals and address changes are routed online before the visitor ever leaves home.
  4. Right window, right timeVisitors are routed to the correct counter and texted when it is their turn, so they can wait in their car or nearby.
  5. Staff where demand will beForecasts by hour, day and season help supervisors open windows and plan shifts before lines form.

Legacy Modernization

Modernize without a risky rebuild.

Many critical public systems are decades old and still work. We protect what works, unlock its data, and replace components only when the business case is clear.

Step 01

Wrap

Expose legacy data and transactions through a secure API gateway, without changing the core system.

  • System and data inventory
  • SOAP to REST and mainframe adapters
  • Identity federation and monitoring
Step 02

Extend

Build modern public-facing and staff experiences, and AI assistants, on top of those APIs.

  • Portals, mobile and chat channels
  • Event queues for outage resilience
  • Quick wins in 90 days
Step 03

Replace

Retire legacy components one at a time, behind stable interfaces the public never notices.

  • Cloud-native or SaaS replacement
  • Data migration and validation
  • Vendor-neutral selection support
